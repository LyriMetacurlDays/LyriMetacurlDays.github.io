The Shield Boost Niagara Effect


Date: 15th May 2026

Rating:  80  (Better).



WFH and working on the shield boost Niagara effect. I made it by setting the colour based on each axis with a formula so if it was under 30 in any units then it would be cut off. Seems good.

The Emperor visited that night, and we watched up to the intro of Episode 3 of ‘IT: Welcome To Derry’. I showed the shield boost Niagara effect and we mentioned that Descent seems like it was made by someone from the future when Niagara was out and that they had drawn sprites based on ones that Niagara could do. So it gave a spooky feeling that maybe my Niagara creations are so eerily much like the real sprites (despite me not being confident that I would be able to replicate them exactly) because the sprites were drawn based on them. In a time loop.
